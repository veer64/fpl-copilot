# Why 2024-25 "punishes every rebuild" — resolved (2026-08-23)

Read-only forensics on the ~34 on-disk 2024-25 paths (p1/wc/fs/p3/p5
families; oracle arms excluded). Script: session scratchpad `why_2425_v2.py`
(methods: baseline placement among all path totals; realized-vs-predicted
"luck" via the verified XI replay; league/squad concentration; per-gw paired
deltas pooled over arms, bucketed by transfer count). Seven prior
appearances of the signature are listed in the task record (chips at three
decays, bench-aware BB, late wildcards, team-news arms A/B/C).

## The four findings

1. **Placement — the smoking gun.** Each season's no-chip baseline ranked
   among all ~34 non-oracle path totals of its own season:
   2023-24 **16/34** (47th pct), 2025-26 **12/34** (35th pct),
   **2024-25 33/34 (97th percentile)** — effectively the maximum draw (the
   one path above it, fs wc4 2385, rides the GW2–12 windfall already
   flagged in p1_opening_log §8). Every 2024-25 comparison ever made was
   measured against a near-maximal draw; in the other seasons the baseline
   sits mid-distribution. Median 2024-25 path: 2193 — the baseline is +169
   above its own season's median path.
2. **Luck.** Realized minus replay-predicted XI points, whole season: the
   2024-25 baseline under-delivers its predictions by only −125 — the
   least-negative of ANY path measured in any season (its own rebuild arms:
   −317/−307; other seasons' baselines: −318/−286). Relative to its arms
   the 2024-25 baseline is ~+190 luckier — the size of the "rebuild
   penalty" itself.
3. **Concentration — the mechanism of the luck.** 2024-25 is the Salah
   season: top player 344 points (vs 244 Palmer / 239 Haaland), and the
   baseline path HELD him for 318 of them — top-1 share of squad points
   13.8% vs 7.2%/8.8% in the other seasons — while also recording the
   project's best captain hit rate. League-wide haul share is actually the
   LOWEST of the three seasons (17.5%): the season is not burstier overall;
   it has one giant concentration that the baseline captured near-perfectly.
4. **Churn buckets — the mechanism test that kills "reshuffle-into-error".**
   Pooled per-gw deltas vs baseline across all wc+fs arms:
   2024-25 arms lose in EVERY bucket — **−4.19/gw in weeks with 0–1
   transfers**, −5.41 with 2+, +0.93 on wildcard weeks (vs +7.8/+17.0 in
   the other seasons). The loss is NOT concentrated where rebuilds happen;
   it is the flat cost of being on any path other than the lucky one. The
   incremental churn penalty (2+ minus 0–1) is small and near-universal:
   −0.44 (2023-24), −1.22 (2024-25), +0.44 (2025-26).

## The answer, plainly

**Primarily (c): an artefact of always comparing against one unusually
lucky baseline path** — a 97th-percentile draw that held and captained a
once-a-decade player-season nearly perfectly. Diverge from it for any
reason (a chip, a wildcard, six team-news reveals) and the path regresses
toward the season's typical level (~2190), booking −100..−200 regardless
of the deviation's merit — visible as a ~4 pts/gw loss even in weeks with
no transfers at all. There IS a genuine model-on-season component (hardest
rotation, weakest start persistence → the churn differential is largest
here at −1.2/gw extra), but it is an order of magnitude too small to
explain the signature, and it appears at smaller size in 2023-24 — a minor
universal effect. The season property that matters is the Salah
concentration, which made one path exceptional, not rebuilds bad.

## Implications for the record (nothing changed here)

- The seven appearances are largely ONE repeated measurement against one
  high draw, not seven independent confirmations. P4 §12's "loses in
  2024-25" rows and p1_opening_log's 2024-25 caveats should be read as
  "deviating from a 97th-percentile path costs its luck premium", not
  "rebuilds are harmful in seasons like this".
- Prediction, falsifiable: re-basing 2024-25 window measurements against a
  median path (e.g. the wc5 family, 2311/2339) would flip many of the
  negative anchors. Not run — recorded as the natural check for whoever
  revisits.
- Caveats: the 34 paths are not independent (shared prefixes, families);
  the percentile is indicative, not inferential. The luck metric carries
  the global winner's-curse bias, netted out by cross-arm comparison.

## The falsifiable prediction — TESTED (2026-08-23): partially confirmed

Method (scratchpad `rebase_2425.py`): re-base each anchor window against
the PER-GAMEWEEK MEDIAN across the season's ~34 non-oracle paths,
excluding paths playing a WC/FH inside the window. (The log's own "wc5
family" suggestion was wrong — those totals sit high-percentile, not
median; the per-gw median pool is the defensible reference and was used.)
Controls: identical treatment of 2023-24 and 2025-26.

**2024-25 (19 negative results re-based): 8 flip positive, ALL 19 shrink,
and every second-half anchor shifts positive by +10..+61** — the inherited
luck premium, concentrated exactly where the baseline's Salah-heavy run
was strongest. Flips: d45 WC1 (−5→+6), d60 WC2 (−22→+5), d60 BB2
(−30→+4), wc-grid GW4 both openings (→+6), GW6 both (−42→+19, −28→+34),
GW7 p1 (−65→+6). Survivors at reduced magnitude: d45 WC2 −38→−26, d45 BB2
−28→−18, d85 BB2 −27→−14, BB-aware −37→−24, GW8 −51/−63→−12/−12,
team-news A/B/C −85/−140/−122 → −24/−78/−60.

**Controls behave for the windowed measurements:** chip-anchor shifts in
2023-24/2025-26 are ±10-ish with MIXED signs (2023-24's late anchors move
DOWN — its baseline was slightly unlucky late), versus 2024-25's
one-signed positive corrections. Two control wc-grid cells flip (2023-24
GW7/GW8) — the method carries ~±10-15 of noise; it does not systematically
flatter what is re-based. **The season-TOTAL re-base (team-news arms) is
the method's weak point in both directions:** control values inflate by
+50..+90 because the fs reference carries chips the median pool mostly
lacks — so the 2024-25 team-news re-based values are optimistic and the
truth lies between original and re-based.

**Verdict: the signature is roughly HALF artefact, and the artefact is the
larger half.** The luck-premium mechanism is confirmed (one-signed
corrections, 8/19 flips, survivors at ~40-60% of original magnitude); a
real residual rebuild penalty remains (d45 WC2/BB2, GW8, team-news arms
still negative after re-basing), consistent with §4's small genuine
churn-cost. Conclusions that should be READ differently (recorded, not
changed): p4_chip_policy_log §12's "loses in 2024-25" rows and §12a/§12b's
GW6-8 "catastrophic" framing (GW6 flips positive when re-based);
p1_opening_log §7's 2024-25 late-wildcard row; teamnews_step5's
−91/−120/−107 (about half is reference luck). The WC1 GW2–3 rule is
UNAFFECTED and if anything strengthened (re-based 2024-25: GW2 +42/+60,
GW5 +52).
